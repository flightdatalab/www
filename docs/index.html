Why Telemetry Normalization Matters

Unmanned aerial vehicles record sensor topics at asynchronous rates—high-frequency IMU samples (400 Hz–1 kHz), medium-rate EKF attitude estimates (50–100 Hz), and lower-rate GPS or battery telemetry (5–10 Hz). Comparing flights across airframes or autopilots requires consistent schemas, units, and monotonic timestamps.

Core Schema Conventions

  • Monotonic Timebase: Unified timestamp_us (microseconds since boot) paired with UTC epoch alignment when GPS lock is valid.
  • SI Physical Units: Meters, meters/second, radians, volts, amperes, and degrees Celsius enforced across all adapters.
  • Columnar Storage: Exportable to Apache Parquet and Arrow tables for zero-copy analytics in Python, DuckDB, and R.

Open Research Datasets Roadmap

Alongside our analysis libraries, Flight Data Lab curates anonymized, schema-validated UAV telemetry datasets to benchmark anomaly detection algorithms and battery discharge models across multi-rotor and fixed-wing unmanned platforms.

Dataset Track Primary Signals Target Use Case Format
FDL-Nominal-Flight IMU, Baro, GPS, RC Out, Battery Baseline flight profile & trajectory modeling Apache Parquet
FDL-Power-Discharge Pack Voltage, Current Draw, Temp, mAh Battery endurance & voltage sag prediction Parquet / CSV
FDL-Anomaly-Bench Labeled Vibration, GPS Drift & Sag Events Evaluating flight log anomaly detectors Parquet + JSON Labels
